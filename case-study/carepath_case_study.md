# CarePath Case Study

## Overview
CarePath is a mental-health access and operations concept designed to help users navigate support options while helping service teams identify demand patterns, wait-time bottlenecks, urgency levels, and follow-up gaps.

## Problem
People seeking mental health support often do not know where to start, what level of care they need, or how urgent their situation is. At the same time, behavioral health systems need better visibility into demand, wait times, referral completion, and access barriers.

## Goal
Design a non-diagnostic access and triage concept that helps users identify next steps while giving service teams operational insight.

## Deliverables
- User journey map
- Triage routing framework
- Mock intake dataset
- Operations dashboard
- Product and process recommendations

## Tools
GitHub, Python, CSV, dashboarding tool, and design/wireframing tools.
