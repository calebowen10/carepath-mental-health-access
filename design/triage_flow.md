# Triage Flow

This triage flow is not diagnostic. It is a routing framework for matching users with appropriate support options.

## Purpose

CarePath uses brief intake responses to route users toward general support pathways. The goal is to reduce confusion, help users identify a next step, and give service teams visibility into demand, urgency, and access barriers.

This framework does not diagnose mental health conditions or replace clinical judgment.

---

## Low Urgency

### User Profile
The user may report:
- General stress
- Mild anxiety or mood concerns
- Low immediate support need
- Interest in coping tools or routine support
- No crisis or safety indicators

### Recommended Support Options
- Self-guided resources
- Peer support
- Routine outpatient appointment
- Digital check-in
- Wellness or coping-skills content

### Example Routing Message
Based on your responses, you may benefit from routine support or self-guided resources. You can explore coping tools, request a routine appointment, or schedule a follow-up check-in.

---

## Moderate Urgency

### User Profile
The user may report:
- Persistent distress
- Difficulty functioning at school, work, or home
- Limited social support
- Worsening symptoms
- Need for help soon, but no immediate crisis indicator

### Recommended Support Options
- Outpatient therapy
- Telehealth appointment
- Care navigator follow-up
- Community behavioral health referral
- Scheduled support within a shorter timeframe

### Example Routing Message
Based on your responses, it may be helpful to connect with a support provider or care navigator. You can request a telehealth appointment, outpatient referral, or follow-up from a support team.

---

## High Urgency

### User Profile
The user may report:
- Crisis indicators
- Safety concerns
- Severe distress
- Urgent need for support
- Feeling unable to wait for routine care

### Recommended Support Options
- Crisis line
- Mobile crisis support
- Urgent behavioral health center
- Emergency support
- Immediate outreach from a support team

### Example Routing Message
Based on your responses, you may need urgent support. If you feel unsafe or at immediate risk, contact emergency services or a crisis line right now. CarePath can also direct you to urgent behavioral health resources.

---

## Access Barriers Considered

CarePath should also account for barriers that may affect whether someone can actually use the recommended support option.

Examples:
- Insurance barrier
- Transportation barrier
- Digital access barrier
- Language/accessibility need
- Location or provider availability
- Preference for in-person, telehealth, peer support, or crisis support

---

## Portfolio Note

This triage framework is not intended to diagnose users. It is a routing and access model designed to help users identify appropriate support pathways and help administrators understand service demand.
