# User Journey Map

This journey map shows how a person might move through CarePath when they are overwhelmed, unsure where to start, and looking for mental health support.

## Journey Flow

User feels overwhelmed  
↓  
User opens CarePath  
↓  
User answers brief intake questions  
↓  
System identifies general urgency/support category  
↓  
User receives recommended next step  
↓  
User chooses an appointment, crisis resource, peer support, telehealth, or follow-up option  
↓  
System tracks referral completion and follow-up status  

## User Problem

People experiencing mental health distress often do not know where to start, what level of support they need, or how urgent their situation is.

## System Problem

Behavioral health providers often lack clean visibility into demand, urgency, wait times, referral completion, and where users drop off before receiving support.

## Why This Matters

The goal is not to diagnose users. The goal is to reduce confusion, route people toward appropriate support options, and help service teams understand where the access process breaks down.
